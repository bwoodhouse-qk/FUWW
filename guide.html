Your first shop with Pink Trolley

Turn your grocery list into supermarket searches, one item at a time. You choose products, add them to your trolley, and check out on the supermarket website.

Open the shopping panel

Once the extension is installed in desktop Chrome, open the puzzle-piece Extensions menu and pin Queerly Kiwi Pink Trolley. Click the pink trolley icon in your toolbar to open the panel beside your shopping tab.

Shop in four steps

  1. Choose your supermarket. Click Shop at Woolworths or Shop at Pak n Save. Log in on the store website and return to the panel. The “Shopping at” label shows which store your searches will use.
  2. Make your list. Paste or type one item per line in the Grocery list box, then click Create list. Your items appear below, with the first item highlighted. Blank lines are ignored.
  3. Search for the first item. Click Start shopping. The store tab shows results for the highlighted item. Choose a product and add it to your trolley on the store website.
  4. Move to the next item. Click Next in the panel to search for the next item. Repeat until you reach the end, then review your trolley and check out on the store website.

For example, paste:

Milk
Bread
Apples

Keep a copy of your list. Pink Trolley holds it only while the panel page stays loaded. Closing or reloading that page loses the list; it is not saved for your next visit.

Go back or skip an item

Previous searches for the previous item. Click any item in the list to jump straight to its search. You can skip an item by clicking Next without adding a product.

The highlighted item and “Item … of …” message show your position. Previous is disabled at the first item and Next at the last. Moving through the list does not record whether you bought anything.

Change your list

Edit the text in the Grocery list box, then click Update list to apply your changes. Until you click it, shopping uses your existing list. Updating does not start a search.

Your current item stays selected when possible, including when you rename it. If you remove it, the next remaining item is selected, or the last item if there is no next one.

Use Create list to start again at the first item. To clear the list, delete all the text and apply the update.

Switch supermarkets

Click the other supermarket's Shop button. Your list and highlighted item stay in place. Click Start shopping to search that item at the newly selected store.

Pink Trolley reuses an existing tab for each store, opening one if needed. Searches replace the page in that tab. Switching stores does not transfer products between supermarket trolleys. A freshly loaded panel defaults to Woolworths.

If something looks wrong

  • No Start shopping button? Enter at least one item and click Create list.
  • Searching at the wrong store? Check the “Shopping at” label, choose the store you want, then click Start shopping.
  • No useful results? Try a simpler item name, click Update list, then Start shopping to search again.
  • Could not open the store? Try the action again. If Chrome requests access to the selected supermarket site, allow it so the extension can find its tab.
  • List disappeared? Paste your copy again and click Create list. Pink Trolley cannot recover a list after the panel page closes or reloads.

Using a keyboard

Use Tab to move between controls. Press Enter or Space on a button or list item to activate it. The first-shop instructions in the panel can also be collapsed or expanded with Enter or Space.
