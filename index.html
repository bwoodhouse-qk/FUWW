Queerly Kiwi Pink Trolley

The delightfully opinionated little extension that helps you shop a pasted grocery list, one item at a time.

First shop? Start here
  1. Choose your supermarket below and log in on its website.
  2. Paste one item per line, then click Create list.
  3. Click Start shopping to search for the highlighted item.
  4. Add your chosen product on the store website, then click Next in this panel.
